Privacy Policy

BRICK: Arcade Defense

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled when you play BRICK: Arcade Defense (“the App”), use its advertising and purchase features, or contact the developer.

1. Who is responsible

The developer responsible for the App is BABEL.SK (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions or requests, contact dev@babel.sk.

Google and Apple also process information when providing the services described below. Their privacy notices explain their respective responsibilities and practices.

2. Information stored on your device

You can play without creating an account or providing your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

The App saves the following information locally:

  • High scores and the dates and sectors associated with them.
  • Campaign progress and checkpoints for continuing a game.
  • Sound, music, and tilt-control preferences.

The developer does not upload these game records or preferences to a developer-operated server. Device backups managed by Apple may include local App data, depending on your device and backup settings.

If you enable tilt controls, the App uses device-motion readings to move the paddle. The App does not save a history of these readings or send them to the developer or advertising services.

3. Advertising and information processed by Google

The App integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. It requests non-personalized ads and does not offer an option to enable personalized advertising.

Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Depending on the service, device settings, and applicable privacy choices, Google and the advertising providers involved in delivering an ad may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device or app identifiers, subject to platform restrictions.
  • Device model, operating system, and technical information.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and other advertising interactions.
  • Crash reports, performance measurements, and diagnostic data.

These services use information to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, maintain service reliability, and detect fraud or abuse.

The App does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. This does not mean that advertising services process no information.

More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of how Google uses information from apps and sites using its services.

4. Consent and privacy choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine whether a privacy message is required and to manage the available advertising privacy choices.

UMP may process approximate location, technical performance information, and interactions with its forms to provide this functionality. It stores privacy-choice information on your device.

Where applicable, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a privacy message provides information and choices concerning advertising-related processing. Available options depend on your region and the applicable message.

The App requests ads only when UMP reports that ad requests are permitted. Gameplay does not require you to accept advertising consent or create an account.

To review or change your choices, open Options → Privacy Choices. This entry is displayed when UMP requires it for your situation. The labels may be translated into your selected language.

Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it through the available privacy controls. Withdrawal applies to future processing and does not affect the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.

5. In-app purchases

The App offers a one-time purchase to remove advertising. Apple processes the purchase through the App Store.

The App receives transaction and entitlement information from Apple's StoreKit service to verify whether the ad-free upgrade is active and to restore previous purchases.

The developer does not receive your payment-card details through this purchase flow. Apple maintains purchase records under its own policies. For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Removing ads does not delete information already processed by advertising services. Deleting the App does not erase your App Store purchase history.

6. Contacting the developer

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include in the message or attachments. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for resolving your request.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the functionality you request, respond to support enquiries, and meet legal obligations.

For support correspondence, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to users and resolving issues, or on steps necessary to provide a requested service, as applicable.

Where consent is legally required for advertising-related processing or access to information on your device, the relevant processing depends on your consent. Google and other service providers explain their own purposes and legal bases in their notices and the applicable consent information.

8. Sharing and international processing

The App uses Google for advertising and consent functionality, and Apple for purchases and platform services. Information you send by email is also handled by the email services used to deliver and manage that correspondence.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy notices describe processing locations and the safeguards used for international transfers where required. Google's information about transfer safeguards is available in its data-transfer frameworks notice.

We do not operate an account-registration service, public leaderboard, or developer-hosted database of gameplay records.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local gameplay data: retained on your device until overwritten during normal use or removed by deleting the App. Offloading the App may retain its data. Backup copies are managed through your Apple backup settings.
  • Support correspondence: retained for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations or resolve a dispute.
  • Advertising and consent information: retained by the relevant providers according to their purposes, retention policies, and legal obligations.
  • Purchase records: maintained by Apple under its own retention policies.

To remove the App's local data, use iOS Settings to delete the App, rather than only removing its Home Screen icon or offloading it. This does not automatically delete records held by Google, Apple, or other service providers.

You can contact us about information you have sent directly to us. For information controlled by Google or Apple, use their privacy controls or contact those providers. We can explain which service is relevant to your request.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, restrict processing, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent.

Send requests concerning information handled by the developer to dev@babel.sk. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity before responding.

You may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority, including the authority in the country where you live or work. If you are in Slovakia, information is available from the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic.

11. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The App Store age rating describes content suitability and is separate from legal requirements concerning children's personal data and consent.

We do not ask children to create accounts or submit personal information. Advertising services may nevertheless process device information as described above.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact dev@babel.sk so that we can investigate and address the request.

12. Security

Local App data is stored within the storage provided by iOS. We use the platform's purchase services and established advertising and consent services. No system can guarantee absolute security for stored or transmitted information.

13. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its HTML does not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process technical request information, including visitors' IP addresses, when serving the website.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about its handling of website visitor data.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date shown above. Where required, material changes will also be communicated through the App or the applicable consent process.
