Support

BRICK: Arcade Defense

Help with gameplay, purchases, and technical issues.

Need help with BRICK: Arcade Defense, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Contact the developer, BABEL.SK, using the email below.

1. Contact support

Email: dev@babel.sk

If the email link does not open your mail app, copy the address into your preferred email service. Please include “BRICK Support” in the subject.

To help us investigate a problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The version of BRICK you are using, if available.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that trigger the issue.
  • A screenshot or short recording, if it helps explain the problem.

Please do not send your Apple Account password, payment-card details, or verification codes. Remove personal information from screenshots before sending them.

2. Restore your Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase. If you already purchased it, open Arcade Shop → Restore Purchases while connected to the internet and using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Labels may be translated into your selected language.

If the upgrade is not recognized or ads remain visible, contact us with the message shown in the game and the steps you tried. You do not need to send payment details or purchase another upgrade to report the issue.

3. Purchase unavailable or still pending

If the shop shows that the upgrade is unavailable, check your internet connection and use Retry when it is available. If a purchase is pending approval, wait for the approval process to finish.

If a purchase screen appears stuck, tell us which step it reached and whether an Apple confirmation or error appeared. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same purchase while it is still being processed.

4. Gameplay and tilt controls

Tilt controls can be enabled in Options → Tilt Control. If movement stops unexpectedly, check whether the game is paused and select Resume before trying again.

When reporting a gameplay problem, include the sector and whether it happened after a reset, a pause, or returning from another app. Please do not delete the game as a first troubleshooting step: doing so can remove locally stored progress and settings.

5. Report an advertisement

To report an inappropriate or broken advertisement, open Options → Report an Ad, or email us with a description and a screenshot if available. You do not need to click the advertisement.

6. Privacy questions

Read our Privacy Policy for information about local game data, advertising, purchases, and support correspondence. You can also send privacy questions to dev@babel.sk.

Advertising privacy controls are available under Options → Privacy Choices when required for your situation.
