Privacy Policy

Animal Match

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled when you or your child play Animal Match (“the App”), a picture memory game for young children, use its in-app purchase, or contact the developer.

1. Who is responsible

The developer responsible for the App is BABEL.SK (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions or requests, contact dev@babel.sk.

2. Summary

  • The App does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The App does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • Everything the App remembers stays on the device.

3. Information stored on the device

The App saves a small number of settings on the device so that it behaves the same way the next time it is opened:

  • Whether music, sound effects and spoken animal names are switched on.
  • Whether the short how-to-play tutorial has already been shown.
  • Whether a celebration for newly unlocked worlds is still due to be shown.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. The App does not keep scores, play history or any other record of how a child plays. Deleting the App removes this information. Device backups managed by Apple may include it, depending on your backup settings.

4. Spoken animal names

When a pair is found, the App can say the animal's name using the speech voices built into iOS and iPadOS. Speech is produced on the device; the App does not record audio and does not send any text or audio to us. The feature can be switched off in the App's settings under “Animal names”.

5. In-app purchase

One world is free. The other worlds can be unlocked with a single one-time purchase. Purchases and restores are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store under your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information; the App only learns from Apple whether the unlock has been purchased. Apple's handling of this information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

The purchase screen is placed behind a parental gate (a simple multiplication question) so that a young child cannot start a purchase on their own. Family Sharing and Ask to Buy settings on the device continue to apply.

6. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children aged about 3 to 7. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Links that leave the App — this Privacy Policy and the support page — are reachable only from the Help screen, which is also behind the parental gate.

7. Contacting us

If you write to us by e-mail, we use your e-mail address and the content of your message only to reply and to fix reported problems. We keep the correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. Your rights

Since the App itself holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete from it. For e-mail correspondence you have the rights granted by applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, such as access, correction and deletion. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; in Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic.

9. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about its handling of website visitor data.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date shown above.
