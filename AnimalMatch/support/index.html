Animal Match

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Animal Match is a picture memory game for children aged about 3 to 7. Turn over two cards; if they show the same animal, they stay face up. Find all the pairs to win. Questions not answered here can be sent to dev@babel.sk.

1. Playing

What do the difficulty levels change?

Easy uses 12 cards, Medium 20 and Hard 28. Easy has no timer, so a child can take as long as they like; the other levels have a time limit. The time a round took is shown on the results screen at every level.

What are the worlds?

Each world has its own animals: Ocean, Insects, Birds, Nearby (farm, garden and forest animals) and Wild. Choose a world in the main menu before pressing Play.

Can the game be paused?

Yes. Use the pause button during a round. The game also pauses when the App goes to the background.

How do I show the tutorial again?

Open Settings and switch on Tutorial. It plays before the next game.

2. Sound

How do I turn off music, sounds or the spoken animal names?

Open Settings. Music, Sounds and Animal names each have their own switch. Animal names are part of the sounds, so they are also silent when Sounds is off.

The animal names are not spoken, or sound wrong.

Names are spoken in the App's language (English, Slovak or Czech) using the speech voices built into iPadOS. Check that Sounds and Animal names are on in the App, that the iPad is not muted, and that a voice for the language is available in iPadOS Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices. If a particular name is pronounced badly, please let us know which one.

3. Unlocking worlds

What is free and what costs money?

The Ocean world is free. A single one-time purchase unlocks all the other worlds. There are no subscriptions, no advertising and no other purchases.

Why does the App ask a multiplication question?

The shop and the Help page are behind a parental gate so that a young child cannot make a purchase or leave the App by accident. An adult types the answer; after a wrong answer there is a short wait before trying again.

I bought the unlock but the worlds are still locked.

Open the shop from the lock button in the main menu and choose Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. With Family Sharing, the purchase is available to family members if it was shared. If Ask to Buy is on, the worlds unlock once the request is approved.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

4. Requirements

  • iPad running iPadOS 26.5 or later.
  • iPad only; the App is not built for iPhone.
  • Available in English, Slovak and Czech; the App follows the iPad's language.
  • No account and no sign-in. The App works offline; an internet connection is needed only for the purchase.
  • VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion are supported.

5. Privacy

The App collects no personal information and has no advertising or tracking. The Privacy Policy describes this in full.

6. Reporting a problem

Write to dev@babel.sk and include:

  • What you did, step by step.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Your iPad model and iPadOS version.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.
