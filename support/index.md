# BRICK: Arcade Defense — Support

Questions, bug reports and anything else about the game:
**[dev@babel.sk](mailto:dev@babel.sk)**

When you write, it helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the
sector you were on. Screenshots are welcome.

## Remove Ads

### I bought Remove Ads and the banner is still there

Open the game, go to **STORE** and tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**. The purchase is
tied to the Apple Account that bought it, so make sure the device is signed in
to that same account. If the banner is still visible, close the game completely
and open it again.

### I got a new iPhone — do I have to buy it again?

No. Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Sign in with the same
Apple Account, open **STORE** and tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**.

### The Arcade Shop shows UNAVAILABLE

That means the App Store could not be reached. Check your connection and tap
**RETRY**. If it keeps failing, try again later — the App Store occasionally
refuses product requests for a short time.

### I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in and
choose the purchase.

## Playing

### Tilt control does not move the paddle

Turn it on first: **OPTIONS → TILT CONTROL**. Tilt works alongside touch — if
your finger is on the screen, the drag takes priority and tilt resumes when you
lift it. Tilt needs the motion sensors of a real iPhone.

### Where did my progress go?

Your campaign progress, checkpoints and high scores are stored on the device
itself. They are not uploaded anywhere and do not sync between devices, so
deleting the game or switching phones starts you over. Your ten best runs are
kept.

### I lost my run when I closed the game

You should not have. BRICK saves your sector, score and lives at the point you
left off — reopen the game and choose **CONTINUE**. If a run genuinely
disappeared, please write to us and say what you were doing at the time.

## Ads and privacy

### How do I change my privacy choices?

**OPTIONS → PRIVACY CHOICES.** This entry appears only where the choice is
legally required, which is why you may not see it in every country.

### I saw an ad that should not be there

**OPTIONS → REPORT AN AD.** It prepares an e-mail with the ad's response ID so
we can identify it. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

### What data does the game collect?

The game itself keeps your scores and progress on your device and uploads
nothing. Advertising is served by Google AdMob and the game always requests
non-personalised ads. The full details are in our
[Privacy Policy](https://0xbabel.github.io/my-game-privacy/).

## Requirements and limitations

- iPhone with **iOS 17.0** or later
- **iPhone only** — the game is not built for iPad
- **Portrait orientation** only
- Available in English, Slovak, Czech, German, Spanish and French
- No account and no sign-in; nothing to register
- VoiceOver is supported, including an adjustable paddle control
